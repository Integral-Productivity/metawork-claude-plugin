# Meta Work Strategy

## Target problem

Across the lifecycle of any project, area of focus, or life domain, we face the
cyclical problem of sensemaking and wayfinding *simultaneously at multiple
levels* — the nested adaptive cycles and holarchy we live inside but rarely see
clearly. Intention and perspective decay silently: the *why* fades, the system
we're in shifts unnoticed, projects outlive their purpose, and the effortful
meta level loses to the dopamine of the object level unless something holds it.
The developmental twist makes it harder still — earlier stages are blind to the
multi-levelness and over-choose; later stages get lost navigating
systems-of-systems and forget to return to grounded experience.

## Our approach

Make the meta level explicit as a single nested artifact — one Meta Work Group
per (scope, subject), nested into a holarchy so multi-level sensemaking is
*structural* — and hold it with a repeating ritual (morning check, retro,
diagnose) so wayfinding is continuous rather than a one-time plan. Integration,
nesting, and cadence are the spine; this rules out the flat one-time plan, the
separate-practices-run-in-parallel, and the standalone destination app.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** The reflective self-manager — someone already running a serious
personal system (often an OmniFocus/PKM power user), hiring Meta Work to keep
purpose, perspective, and developmental edge alive across the whole portfolio of
projects, areas, and identities, catching drift before it costs them.

**Secondary:** Coaches and practitioners (e.g. Integral Productivity) who would
run the practice with clients — a v2 surface, not a v1 product driver.

## Key metrics

- **Cadence adherence** — % of active Meta Work Groups that get their perspective
  re-check on schedule (morning checks done / due). Leading, weekly; measured in
  OmniFocus task completion / markdown dated blocks.
- **Graceful endings rate** — share of projects intentionally closed or dropped
  as outlived-purpose, vs. silently abandoned. Lagging, can regress; measured in
  retro/diagnose records.
- **Drift caught & acted on** — count of decays (faded *why*, shifted system,
  outlived purpose) noticed and acted on per period, rather than discovered too
  late. Lagging; measured in diagnose/retro.
- **Reflection : action (praxis) balance** — the polarity balance between
  perspective-holding and execution; healthy = sustained tension, not collapse
  to either pole. Surfaced in metawork-retro output.

## Tracks

### The ritual engine (skills)

set-up plus the recurring re-check skills (morning, retro, diagnose) that make
cadence real.

_Why it serves the approach:_ It's the engine that converts perspective-holding
from a one-time plan into a repeated loop — the heart of "re-checked on cadence."

### Persistence & nesting

Backends (OmniFocus, markdown; later Obsidian, Things, Notion) plus the
schema/data model that expresses the holarchy via parent references.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Delivers "one nested artifact that lives where you
already work" — the structural half of multi-level sensemaking.

### Coaching & development

The Socratic scholar and diagnose, holding the developmental layer — positive
disintegration, vertical-stage calibration, remember-and-revolt.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Keeps the practice engaging the developmental edge
rather than reinforcing old footholds — the wayfinding dimension.
